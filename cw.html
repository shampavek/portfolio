01 overview

The pursuit of corporate whimsy

Climate week is a sustainability event organized by the Environmental Policy and Planning Commission. Over 40,000 students are exposed to a space to participate in sustainable efforts and learn more about climate action!

I developed the visual language across print, signage, and digital touchpoints, creating a system that could scale across different sizes, environments, and audiences.

02 the challenge

What is corporate whimsy???

My creative brief for this project was encapsulated in two words: corporate whimsy. My stakeholders wanted a branding identity that was timeless, whimsical while still following the stringent brand guidelines the University maintained.

constraint

Information needed to remain legible across different physical sizes and viewing distances.

opportunity

Create a visual system that could turn functional information into something recognizable and engaging.

03 context

Understanding the environment.

Before designing the final assets, I looked at where and how people would encounter the work. This meant considering audience, physical space, viewing distance, accessibility, and the amount of information that could realistically be processed at once.

audience

Who is actually interacting with this piece, and what do they need to know?

environment

Where will the design live, and how does that environment affect readability?
