01 overview

Making complex things easier to understand.

tldr: Integrative Privacy helps industry leaders like Uber, KFC, Taco Bell, and GoFundMe implement government-compliant data practices. The legacy marketing site lacked clear calls-to-action (CTAs) for partnerships and relied on complex GitHub workflows, blocking non-technical teams from publishing timely updates.

As the sole web developer, I led the migration to Webflow: establishing an easy-to-use CMS, fixing visual hierarchy, and improving site accessibility. This eliminated developer dependency for content updates while scaling the site's overall reach and conversions.

02 the problem

Integrative Privacy’s initial digital presence was outdated and muddled.

As I worked with the founding team, I realized that the mission of the company was difficult to articulate for non-technical clients who may need the company. Their website had vague information on previous client work and services offered, creating a potential disconnect between new clients and harnessing IP's full potential.
